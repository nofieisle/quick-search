Privacy Policy – Quick Search

Last updated: March 2026

Overview

Quick Search is a Chrome extension that lets you search selected text on Google using the keyboard shortcut Alt+S. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy describes what data the extension handles.

Data Collection

Quick Search does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. No analytics, no telemetry, no third-party tracking.

How the Extension Works

When you press Alt+S, the extension reads the text you have selected on the current tab and opens a new tab with a Google search for that text (https://www.google.com/search?q=<selected text>). The selected text is passed directly to Google as part of the URL — the same as typing it into the Google search bar yourself. The extension does not send this text to any other server.

Permissions Explained

  • activeTab – Required to read the text you have selected on the current page when you press the keyboard shortcut.
  • scripting – Required to run a small script on the current tab that retrieves the selected text.

Third-Party Services

The extension opens Google Search (google.com) in a new tab. Your use of Google Search is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. Quick Search has no affiliation with Google.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the revision date.
